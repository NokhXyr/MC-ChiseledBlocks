<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks-Create/title-start-en.gif" alt="Getting Started" width="800"></h3>
<p><span style="color:#d19a3a;font-size:20px"><strong>1.</strong></span> Install Chiseled Blocks and Create 6.0.10 or newer.</p>
<p><span style="color:#d19a3a;font-size:20px"><strong>2.</strong></span> Place each machine two blocks above a belt or a depot, one block of air between.</p>
<p><span style="color:#d19a3a;font-size:20px"><strong>3.</strong></span> Turn it with a shaft through its sides and send blocks along the belt.</p>
</div>
