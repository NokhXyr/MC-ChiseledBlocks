<p><span style="font-size:18px;color:#e5e5e5"><strong>Chiseled Blocks</strong> lets you chisel any block into pixels, 16 to a side, and mix materials inside a single block.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:20px;color:#3a8fe0"><em>Chisel any block, pixel by pixel.</em></span></p>
<p><strong>Inspired by <a href="https://github.com/ChiselsAndBits/Chisels-and-Bits" style="color:#3a8fe0">Chisels &amp; Bits</a>, written from scratch.</strong></p>
</div>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/title-features-en.gif" alt="What It Does" width="800"></h3>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/carve.gif" alt="A calcite block chiseled into a stair, stroke by stroke, then a blue pixel placed above it" width="800"></p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/icons/chisel.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#3a8fe0;font-size:18px"><strong>Chisel:</strong></span> single pixel, line, plane, cube or sphere, from 1 to 8 pixels.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/icons/bag.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#3a8fe0;font-size:18px"><strong>Pixels and bag:</strong></span> every pixel is kept; place them back by hand, in lines, planes or boxes.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/icons/pattern.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#3a8fe0;font-size:18px"><strong>Patterns:</strong></span> copy a chiseling, print it again or carve with it.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/icons/wrench.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#3a8fe0;font-size:18px"><strong>Tools:</strong></span> mirror, wrench, saw, tape measure, and undo for the last 64 actions.</p>
</div>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/title-speed-en.gif" alt="Built For Speed" width="800"></h3>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/speed.gif" alt="16,000 chiseled blocks: playable after 1 second, 617 fps flying over, saved in 67 ms" width="800"></p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/icons/stack-2.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#3a8fe0;font-size:18px"><strong>Shared meshes:</strong></span> identical chiselings share one mesh and one collision shape.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/icons/wifi.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#3a8fe0;font-size:18px"><strong>Light updates:</strong></span> an edit rebuilds its section once and sends only what changed.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/icons/puzzle.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#3a8fe0;font-size:18px"><strong>Compatible:</strong></span> checked with Sodium, Iris, ModernFix, WorldEdit and KubeJS.</p>
</div>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Images@main/MC-ChiseledBlocks/title-start-en.gif" alt="Getting Started" width="800"></h3>
<p><span style="color:#3a8fe0;font-size:20px"><strong>1.</strong></span> Craft an iron chisel and left click a block to carve it.</p>
<p><span style="color:#3a8fe0;font-size:20px"><strong>2.</strong></span> Hold <code>Left&nbsp;Alt</code> for the tool menu: mode, size, mirror, undo.</p>
<p><span style="color:#3a8fe0;font-size:20px"><strong>3.</strong></span> Keep a pixel bag in your inventory and right click with it to place pixels.</p>
</div>
