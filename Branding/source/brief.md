# Chiseled Blocks: brand brief

## The project

Chiseled Blocks is a NeoForge 1.21.1 Minecraft mod by NokhXyr. It lets players chisel any block
into pixels (16 x 16 x 16 per block), mix materials inside one block, copy and reprint chiselings
with patterns, mirror, rotate, saw and measure them. It is a clean rewrite of the idea of Chisels &
Bits, built for frame rate and stability: in the stress runs it stays playable where Chisels & Bits
freezes. An optional addon, Chiseled Blocks: Create, mass-produces pixels on Create belts.

## For whom

Builders on servers and in modpacks (the author's own Arcadia packs first), and pack makers who
need a chiseling mod that does not cost frames.

## Personality

Precise, crafted, fast.

## The one thing the logo must say

A plain block, carved down to the pixel. The result of chiseling, not the tool: the blue pixel is
the unit of the mod.

## Constraints

- Square mark that holds at 16 px (CurseForge and Modrinth show the avatar at 96 px and smaller),
  in one color, and reversed on dark.
- Pixel art, in the game's own style (the user chose it), drawn from the mod's own materials.
- Name as written: Chiseled Blocks. Tagline: Chisel any block, pixel by pixel.
- Colors from the mod: the blue of its tools, tool menu and admin panel (#3a8fe0 family), the stone
  of the reference blocks, the steel of the chisel.

## Avoid

- A diagonal chisel as the icon (the category cliché, see the landscape).
- Anything from Mojang (the Minecraft logo, its font, its art) or from Chisels & Bits.
- Pillow shading, gradients or glow inside the mark.

Clichés: five of the eight chiseling mods show a diagonal chisel. Names close to ours exist: Many
Chiseled Blocks X (CurseForge), Chiseled (Modrinth), Chiseled Bricks.

The opening: no tool at all. Show the block after the cut, with its stepped notch and the one blue
pixel that left it. Nobody in the category draws the result of chiseling; it also tells Chiseled
Blocks apart from the lookalike names, which all draw a chisel or a plain textured block.
